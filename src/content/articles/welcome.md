I spent a decade learning to lift heavy things, and the last few years helping other people do the same. This site is where I'll put the stuff that doesn't fit in a one-off coaching conversation — the programming principles, the technique cues, the mental models that actually hold up under a loaded bar.

A few things I want to write about soon:

- **Autoregulation that doesn't suck**. RPE is a tool, not a religion. When it's useful, when it isn't, and what to do when the bar feels different than the number says.
- **Peaking without panicking**. The last four weeks before a meet matter, but not for the reasons most people think.
- **Why your warm-ups are too long**. (Probably.)

If you want to see the training logger I build on, [PowerTrack](/powertrack) is live. If you want to work with me, the [coaching page](/coaching) tells you how.

Welcome. Thanks for reading.
